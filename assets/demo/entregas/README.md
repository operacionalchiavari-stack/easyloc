# Exemplos de entrega de premiações

Modo: ferramenta nativa imagegen (built-in), sem CLI. Imagens geradas por IA de pessoas fictícias, usadas somente na simulação quando não há fotos de entregas cadastradas. Os registros reais da empresa não são alterados.

Arquivos finais nesta pasta: rafael.png, ana.png, carlos.png e mariana.png.

## rafael.png

Prompt final:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: sample photo for an internal employee award gallery, not a product catalog. Create a natural, candid documentary photograph of two fictional adult Brazilian coworkers at a small furniture workshop, during a modest celebration of achieving a monthly team goal. One coworker is handing a present to the award recipient. Their faces, the recipient's happy expression, and the gift must be clearly visible. Simple unbranded navy polo shirts, warm daylight, ordinary workshop with chairs in the softly blurred background, believable hands and anatomy. Horizontal 4:3 composition, waist-up medium shot, both faces and gift fully inside the frame with comfortable margins. No text, no logos, no watermark. The scene must depict the employee actually receiving their gift, not just an isolated product. The recipient is a smiling brown-skinned man in his thirties with short curly dark hair; a woman colleague hands him an unbranded portable Bluetooth speaker gift box. He holds the speaker box with both hands.
